The leak of Claude Code from Anthropic was one of the most talked-about AI incidents in 2026. However, beyond the leaked source code, one particular feature caught the attention of developers and analysts:

## What Was the AI "Buddy"?

The leaked code referenced functionality resembling:

- a persistent AI assistant  
- a personalized AI agent  
- a digital companion  

This was not a typical chatbot. The AI buddy was designed to:

- operate in an **always-on mode**  
- remember user context over time  
- provide more personalized and human-like interactions
